# Domain glossary

## Mail account

The mailbox identity and its available read, send, draft, and trash capabilities.

## Connection profile

The validated incoming or outgoing mail connection settings required to reach a mail account.

## Read capability

Permission to inspect mailbox health, folders, message summaries, and message content.

## Send capability

Permission to send a message or create a draft as the mail account identity. It is disabled unless explicitly enabled.

## Trash capability

Permission to move one message into the mail account's unique trash folder. It is disabled unless explicitly enabled and never permanently deletes or expunges mail.

## Mutation policy

The independent enablement and rate limit governing one mutating capability.
